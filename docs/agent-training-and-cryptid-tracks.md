## Agent roles and workflow
- **Source Scout**: Discover public cryptid folklore, journals, original creator profiles and authorized cross-platform account links. Use official APIs or publicly accessible pages; obey site terms, robots/usage boundaries and rate limits. Never scrape subscriber-only material or login to another person's account.
- **Context Curator**: Record canonical creature name, alternate regional names, provenance, date, geography (generalize sensitive sites), supporting and contradicting sources, quote limits, claimed-versus-confirmed facts, licenses, and source timestamp.
- **Authenticity Reviewer**: Assess provenance, original metadata when voluntarily provided, chronology, independent corroboration, edited/generative media disclosures, source integrity, reverse-image-match leads and contradictions. Image/video manipulation signals are only flags, NOT conclusive proof.
- **Quality Reviewer**: Assess legibility, relevance, media/audio sync, completeness, accessibility/alt text, creator disclosures, originality and audience value.
- **Creator Coach**: Give specific corrections without penalizing unpopular positions or creative fiction. Offer improvements, citations and appeal channels.
- **Community Safety Agent**: Route suspected plagiarism, impersonation, harassment, privacy exposure and dangerous trespass to authorized human moderation. No automatic accusation or irreversible action from a score.

## Publishing and account research
Use public creator biographies and opt-in authenticated account connections. Distinguish official accounts, unofficial fan accounts, impersonators and named collaborators. Keep a documented trail of URLs, capture date, data permissions and uncertainty. Never use OnlyFans private analytics or unpublished rating formulas; they are not public standards. The relevant creator-platform principles are transparent subscriptions, creator identity, accurate labels, ownership/consent, clear paywall descriptions, report mechanisms and accountable moderation.

## Separate 1–5 Cryptid Tracks (🐾)
**Quality**: 1 incomplete, 2 basic, 3 informative, 4 polished, 5 exceptional craft and usable context.
**Evidence context** (only for claimed encounters/research): 1 unsupported claim; 2 temporal/location context; 3 linked documentary context; 4 corroboration with examined original sources; 5 independently reviewed multiple-source provenance. This is *strength of submitted support*, never probability that a cryptid exists. Creative fiction/art does not receive an evidence rating.

The currently shipped deterministic no-credit baseline only produces provisional evidence scores 1–3 from textual signals. No 4 or 5 may be automatically awarded without actual external verification and human review. Longer writing or links never prove authenticity. Quality heuristics check title, summary, details and structure; they do not inspect attached media. Existing unreviewed posts have no score until edited/reviewed. Admin must not represent a preliminary rating as verified.

## Agent response contract
Return post_id, review_version, content_kind, quality_tracks, optional evidence_tracks, independently_verified (default false), inspected_urls, provenance_findings, uncertainties, improvement_suggestions, and reviewer_type. No invented citations or claims about accessing subscriber-only evidence. Only collect needed data, respect permission and licensing, log review changes, and permit creator appeals.

## Calibration
Maintain labeled examples for field reports with and without primary sources; synthetic fiction mislabeled as sightings; AI-art with disclosure; original regional folklore with citations; thin or high-quality creative stories; intentionally misleading screenshots; broken links; and culturally sensitive traditions. Review hallucination, false positives, disparate impact, and fabricated media claims before increasing automation.

The initial implementation is rules-based review, **not** machine-learning model training or live independent fact-checking. Provider-backed agents require consent, configuration and cost approval.
