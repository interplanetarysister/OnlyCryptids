# Platform rights and enforcement boundaries

Owner instruction: assume appropriate copyright paperwork will be obtained before publication. The project records this as an owner-planned launch step, not proof that a registration has already issued or that any filing grants rights outside its lawful scope.

## Asset-specific protection

- Original human-authored source code, writing, art and protectable arrangement: keep authored source and version history, identify claimed portions and exclude third-party/public-domain materials from ownership claims. Record actual copyright registrations when available.
- Only Cryptids name, logo and source-identifying elements: conduct clearance and record any actual trademark applications/registrations and their territory/classes. Do not show the registered symbol without the applicable registration. A registration does not automatically make any similar service infringing.
- Distinctive nonfunctional presentation: preserve evidence for professional evaluation of any applicable trade-dress rights; do not claim every ordinary sidebar, feed or subscription button is exclusive.
- Confidential implementation/business material: keep it private and apply contributor/vendor confidentiality and access controls. Publicly disclosed ideas cannot simply be relabeled secret.
- Qualifying technical inventions: obtain specialist patent evaluation if there is an actual novel technical invention. Do not represent this business concept or a future filing as an issued patent covering all cryptid monetization.

## Enforcement workflow

Record the specific protected asset, ownership/license authority, original dates, alleged copied material and URLs, side-by-side evidence, applicable registration/territory if any, and review status. Preserve evidence without scraping private accounts or evading access controls. Have an authorized person evaluate substantial copying, confusing branding or confidentiality breach before any external claim, takedown or legal threat. Do not send automated accusations merely because another app monetizes cryptid content. No external notices or legal filings have been sent.

Contractual restrictions apply to actual agreeing parties; they do not bind every independent developer worldwide. Respect open-source licenses, fair use and statutory exceptions. Do not add a universal noncompete or state that competing cryptid platforms need Only Cryptids' permission merely to exist.

## Legal basis checked

U.S. Copyright Office: https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html — copyright does not protect ideas, concepts, systems, or methods. Filing paperwork does not remove this limit.

Copyright licensing and recordation: https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html and https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ12.pdf

Electronic signature definitions/validity: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:15%20section:7006%20edition:prelim) and https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:15%20section:7001%20edition:prelim)

The signing implementation supports clear assent and retention, but does not guarantee enforceability in every jurisdiction. Final operator identity, eligibility, local legal rules, published agreement and real deployed verification remain required.
