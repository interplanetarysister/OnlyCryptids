# Community and Suffering implementation

Owner: Interplanetary Association. Current credit name: Suffering. One whole unit costs 25 US cents; all arithmetic uses integers. The catalog name can change centrally without renaming accounting fields. This is a service-credit design, not a representation that a lawful sweepstakes program is operating.

Creators can configure subscription offers and individually priced premium posts. Offers describe 30-day access to general subscriber-only content; separately priced posts are excluded. No automatic renewal is authorized. Each quote resolves the price, offer revision, beneficiary and period on the server. Changed prices apply to future purchases, not existing agreed access. Purchases are deliberately unavailable until verified PayPal funding, transaction idempotency, refund handling and atomic spending/earnings accounting are connected. Never enable spending by trusting a browser balance or payment-success callback.

Platform-owned profiles are The Pine Watch (forests), Midnight Moth (winged cryptids and art), and Beneath the Surface (aquatic cryptids). Their subscription beneficiary is the platform owner, Interplanetary Association; the stated use is moderation costs. This is an earnings designation, not an executed money transfer or promise that a payment processor has accepted the arrangement. The public PayPal account identifier from the owner is not proof of an authenticated merchant connection. Other creators are separate beneficiaries. No withdrawals or payouts are implemented by this batch.

Ordinary members may post free content and comments without a creator subscription or monetization MFA. Required content-rights consent is separate from login and remains mandatory for original member submissions. Platform-owned editorial content managed by an administrator does not require the operator to assign rights to itself, but publishing still requires the rights confirmation. Paid offers/content require a current, token-bound server verification record from a future trusted MFA integration. Users cannot create that record through the client. Enrollment is not connected, and paid activation is unavailable. Base44's mandatory initial signup email verification is distinct from a recurring login second factor; it has not been bypassed.

Communities have public rules, joinable membership, an owner, and appointed moderators. Only an owner/platform administrator can promote or demote moderators. Moderators can remove content, lock or pin discussions, review reports and ban ordinary members within their own community. They cannot control another community or appoint themselves. Bans block joining, posting, replying and voting. Platform-directed reports are invisible to community moderation queues and cannot be dismissed through community APIs. Platform administrators retain oversight. Reports preserve a snapshot for review; visibility changes are audited.

Comments/replies render original text with safe internal content links. No HTML or remote embed is executed. A shared link does not grant access: protected destinations perform their own authorization checks. Links support post, discussion and community identifiers and reject external hosts, script URLs and malformed identifiers.

Open operational work includes database-enforced username/community/vote uniqueness, rate limits, durable vote ranking across all pages, broader feed pagination, MFA enrollment, legal configuration, media storage and the financial integrations listed above. Existing legacy lowercase entity metadata is not deleted by this source consolidation; client CRUD remains denied and code uses canonical SDK entity names.
