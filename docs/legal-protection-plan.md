# Protection and ownership plan

Draft implementation requirements, not a legal opinion or completed filing.

## Scope of protection

Protect eligible original code, writing, artwork, branding, confidential information, and contractual rights. Do not claim exclusive ownership of the general cryptid subscription-platform idea, facts, public-domain folklore, or independent competing creations. AI-generated material is not assumed copyrightable.

## Platform owner

Configure the actual legal owner, legal contact, address, copyright agent, policy versions, effective dates, and publication year. Do not infer these from another project. Use a copyright footer only after these facts are verified. Do not claim registered trademarks, patents, DMCA registration, or safe-harbor status without evidence.

Conduct comprehensive clearance for Only Cryptids, considering similarity to existing creator-platform brands. Name availability has not been established. Keep brand strings/assets replaceable.

Preserve source/version history, original asset files, human contribution records, dependency licenses and third-party notices. Review provider ownership terms. Prepare contributor assignment and confidentiality agreements for review and signatures, with exclusions for pre-existing and third-party materials. Unexecuted templates do not transfer rights.

## Creator rights

The current required copyright assignment governs transferable rights in covered original content. Preserve attribution, the accepted revenue share and mandatory rights. Model training remains separately optional. Subscriber access does not grant redistribution rights.

## Security and enforcement

Restrict administrative access, private originals, business documents, and secrets. Use server-side authorization, short-lived media delivery, optional watermarks, rate limits and audit logs. Preserve upload hashes and revisions as documentation, not conclusive ownership proof. Strip served GPS metadata by default. Do not promise prevention of screenshots or all piracy.

Implement accurate notice/counter-notice intake, prompt review, creator notifications, repeat-infringer handling, appeals, retention, and required response periods. Distinguish ordinary reports from legally sufficient copyright notices. Do not automatically treat any allegation as ownership proof or require a final court judgment before reviewing a valid notice.

## Policies to finalize

Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Creator Agreement, Community Guidelines, Copyright/IP Policy, payment and cancellation terms, and contractor agreements. Preserve mandatory consumer rights. Do not select an invented jurisdiction or promise a blanket liability waiver.
